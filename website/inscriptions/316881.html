AI Analysis Summary

This inscription from Pergamon (34-29 BC) records the enrollment of ephebes into the civic training system. It lists the presiding magistrates, including the Prytanis, Priest, and Gymnasiarch. A notable feature is the mention of the god Soter Asclepius as the gymnasiarch, a common practice where a deity was symbolically appointed to the office. The ephebes are listed by name and patronymic, often grouped by their respective tribes (phylai) such as Cadmeis and Pelopis. The term 'eutaktoi' indicates that these young men were recognized for their orderly conduct.
