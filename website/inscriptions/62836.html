AI Analysis Summary

This inscription from Delos documents the financial administration of construction projects, likely within the sanctuary or on sacred property. It specifies payments made to contractors for tasks like building walls and roofing, all performed under the authority of the architect and the overseers. The funds are managed by the sacred officials, specifically Lysander and Alpines. The text uses standard accounting terminology such as installments and remaining balances.
