AI Analysis Summary

This extensive Athenian decree from the early 1st century BC (Archonship of Lycomedes) concerns the restoration and protection of sacred lands (temene) and sanctuaries (hiera) that had been illegally occupied or sold. It provides a detailed topographical and religious survey of Attica, mentioning numerous historical figures like Themistocles and Solon, and landmarks such as the Panathenaic Stadium and the harbor of Zea. The decree mandates the recovery of these lands, their leasing for revenue, and the performance of ancestral sacrifices.
