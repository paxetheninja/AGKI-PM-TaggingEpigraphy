AI Analysis Summary

This inscription from Megalopolis, dated to the late 2nd century BC, documents a complex legal dispute involving the city and individuals like Aristonymus. The conflict centers on land tenure, specifically the leasing of territory, the adjudication of boundaries (horoi), and allegations of illegal seizure or theft (sylos). It provides valuable administrative data, including the price of land in drachmae and a list of prominent citizens involved in the legal process.
