AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a comprehensive record of the Theseia festival in Athens during the archonship of Phaedrias (155/4 BC). It begins with a decree honoring the agonothetes Miltiades of Marathon for his exceptional management and personal financial contributions to the festival, including the organization of the procession, sacrifices, and games. The second part of the inscription provides a detailed list of victors in various categories, ranging from musical and heraldic contests to military drills and traditional athletic events like the stadion and pankration.
