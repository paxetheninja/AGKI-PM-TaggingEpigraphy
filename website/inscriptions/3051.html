AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is an Athenian decree from 229/8 BC, the year of the archon Heliodorus (IG II² 834). It is a significant historical document that honors Timosthenes of Paiania, the grandfather of the orator Demosthenes. The text details his contributions to the 'common salvation' and 'freedom' of Athens, mentioning military trophies on land and sea. It follows the standard administrative format of the period, including a detailed list of the proedroi (presiding officers) from various demes and tribes, and grants the honoree high privileges such as maintenance in the Prytaneion and dowries for his daughters.
