AI Analysis Summary

This inscription, known as the 'Delian Accounts' of 374/3 BC, is a major administrative document recording the financial activities of the Athenian-led Amphictyony on Delos. It details the income from interest on loans to various Cycladic cities and individuals, rents from sacred properties, and the expenditures for the sanctuary of Apollo, including religious festivals, prizes, and building maintenance. It provides critical evidence for the economic and political control Athens exerted over the Delian sanctuary during the Second Athenian League.
