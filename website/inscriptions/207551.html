AI Analysis Summary

This is an honorific decree of the Thessalian Koinon (League) dating to the mid-2nd century BC. It honors Aristokles of Larisa for his judicial services, specifically his role as an arbitrator in disputes between the Thessalians and the Perrhaebians, conducted according to a pre-existing treaty (symbolon). The text provides a detailed list of federal officials in the preamble, including the strategos, hipparchos, and secretaries of the synedroi.
