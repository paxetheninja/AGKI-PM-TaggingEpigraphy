AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a decree issued by a religious association (thiasotai) in Athens during the archonship of Glaukippos (c. 273/2 BC). It honors a group of officials—including the treasurer, overseer, secretary, checking clerk, and record-keeper—for their diligent management of the association's affairs. Specifically, they are praised for providing burial funds (taphikon) to deceased members according to the law, managing a surplus of 1220 drachmae, and maintaining piety toward the gods. The decree orders the publication of the text on a stone stele and allocates 30 drachmae for a sacrifice and an offering.
