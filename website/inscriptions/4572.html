AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a multi-year record of the Athenian Pythais to Delos, listing the magistrates, priests, and other officials involved in the administration of the sacred procession and the offering of the firstfruits (aparche) to Apollo. It covers several archonships from the late 2nd to early 1st century BCE and provides a detailed prosopography of the Athenian elite during this period.
