AI Analysis Summary

The inscription contains multiple records of manumission payments made to the city of Chyretiai in Thessaly. Each entry follows a standard formula: dating by the local treasurer and the federal strategos, naming the freed person and their former owner, and recording the payment of the legal fee (15 staters in the earlier period, 22.5 denarii in the Roman period). The text shows signs of damage and reconstruction, as indicated by the brackets.
