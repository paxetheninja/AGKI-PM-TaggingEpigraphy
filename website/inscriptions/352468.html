AI Analysis Summary

This inscription from Priene records the sale of the priesthood of Poseidon Helikonios to a son of Sosibios. It is a complex document combining a contract of sale with a civic decree. It details the extensive privileges granted to the priest, such as tax exemption (ateleia), front-row seating (proedria) at all contests, the right to wear a golden crown and headband (strophiskos), and public meals (sitesis) at the Prytaneion and the Panionion. It also specifies the priest's obligations regarding sacrifices and provides legal protections against the revocation of these rights, including a conditional curse (exoleia) against violators.
