AI Analysis Summary

This is a complex separation curse (a 'trennung' defixio) from the 3rd century AD. It targets the relationship between Vetrubius Felix and Valeria Quadratilla, asking the spirits and chthonic deities to destroy their affection and friendship, and to cause Felix to forget his wife. The text is notable for its use of magical names (voces magicae) like Iao and Iabezebuth, and its detailed genealogical identification of the targets to ensure the curse reaches the correct individuals.
