AI Analysis Summary

This complex Severan-era inscription from Pergamon serves as a votive dedication for the imperial family and records the restoration of a statue of Dionysus by a philosopher-priest. It is notable for the damnatio memoriae of Geta and the extensive list of synod officials who were also distinguished victors in various musical and theatrical competitions across the empire.
