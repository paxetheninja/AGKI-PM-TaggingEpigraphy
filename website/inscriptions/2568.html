AI Analysis Summary

This is a well-known Athenian honorific decree dating to the archonship of Aristophantos (330/29 BC). It honors Eudemos of Plataea for his significant contribution of 1,000 yokes of oxen for the construction of the Panathenaic Stadium and Theater under the administration of Lykourgos. The text provides a detailed look at the intersection of private liturgy/donation and public monumental construction in 4th-century Athens. The decree grants Eudemos the right to own land and a house (enktesis), which was a significant privilege for a non-citizen.
