AI Analysis Summary

This is an Athenian honorific decree dating to 333/2 BC, found at the Amphiareion in Oropos. It honors Pytheas of Alopeke for his exemplary management of public fountains, specifically mentioning new constructions at the sanctuary of Ammon and the Amphiareion. The text provides detailed administrative information, including the archon, secretary, and the financial allocation for the production of the stelae. The second part of the text is highly fragmentary.
