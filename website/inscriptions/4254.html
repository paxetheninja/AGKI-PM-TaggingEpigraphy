AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a classic Athenian ephebic list dating to the reign of the Emperor Trajan (post 102 AD). It records the names of the ephebes and their supervising officials, organized by the months of the Attic calendar. The text is notable for its detailed list of gymnasiarchs and other civic officials like the strategos and archon, providing insight into the administrative and religious life of the Athenian ephebeia during the Roman period.
