AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a complex Athenian administrative record from the late 4th century BC. It functions as both a sacred inventory of the treasuries of Athena and other gods and a financial account of the state. It documents the weight and provenance of dedicated items, such as those from Roxane, and records the allocation of public funds (in Alexander's coinage) for military purposes, such as the procurement of timber for the navy, all authorized by official decrees of the Council and the People.
