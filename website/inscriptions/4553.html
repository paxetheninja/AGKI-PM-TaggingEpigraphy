AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a victor list from the Panathenaic Games in Athens, dating to the early 2nd century BC. It records winners from across the Greek world, including prominent members of the Ptolemaic and Attalid royal dynasties, as well as elite women who owned winning chariot teams. The text is organized by age groups (boys, beardless youths, and men) and covers both gymnic (athletic) and hippic (equestrian) events.
