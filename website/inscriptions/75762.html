AI Analysis Summary

This inscription from Anaphe is a detailed honorific decree for Archonides, son of Socles. It outlines his services to the city and the resulting honors: a golden crown, a marble statue to be placed in the city or the sanctuary of Apollo Asgelatas, and a public funeral. It also specifies that his honors should be proclaimed annually during major local festivals such as the Asgelaia, Theudaisia, and Taurophonia. The text is fragmentary, with the upper section missing, but the legal formulas and the final vote count of ninety-five votes are preserved.
