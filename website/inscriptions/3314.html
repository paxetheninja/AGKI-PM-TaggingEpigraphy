AI Analysis Summary

This inscription preserves a significant letter from Empress Plotina and Emperor Hadrian (dated to 121 AD) addressed to the Epicurean school in Athens. It grants the head of the school (the diadochos) the right to appoint a successor who may be a non-Roman citizen (a Greek) and allows the use of a Greek-style will for this purpose. It is a crucial document for understanding the legal status of philosophical schools in the Roman Empire and the personal interest of the imperial family in Athenian intellectual life.
