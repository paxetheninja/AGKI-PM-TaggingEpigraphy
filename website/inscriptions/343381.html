AI Analysis Summary

This inscription from Dyme (Achaia) dates to the late 3rd century BC and records a collective grant of citizenship to a large group of individuals. The preamble identifies the eponymous magistrate (theokolos) and other high-ranking officials of the city. The grant was awarded as a reward for military service during a war (likely the Social War of 220-217 BC), specifically for fighting alongside the city and helping to preserve its safety. The text is a significant administrative and honorific document, providing a detailed list of new citizens and their patronymics.
