AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a naval inventory from Athens, dated to 334/3 BC. It records the administrative and financial oversight of the Athenian fleet, including the handover of triremes and equipment between curators, the legal responsibilities of trierarchs, and the collection of debts and fines for missing gear. It provides a detailed look at the logistics and legal framework of the Athenian navy during the Lycurgan period.
