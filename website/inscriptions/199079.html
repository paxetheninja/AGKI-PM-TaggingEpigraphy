AI Analysis Summary

This is a Hellenistic decree from the Cretan city of Biannos responding to an embassy from Teos. It reaffirms the kinship (syngeneia) between the two cities, recognizes the asylia of Teos as a city sacred to Dionysus, and grants the Teians equal citizenship and military protection. The text includes standard diplomatic formulas, such as the invitation to the Prytaneion and the appointment of proxenoi. The dating is provided by the magistrate Dexios and the month Eleusinios.
