AI Analysis Summary

This inscription from Delos is a meticulous administrative inventory of sacred vessels, likely from the sanctuary of Apollo. It records various types of cups (poteria) and bowls (phialai), often named after their donors or styles (e.g., stesileion, philonideion, mikytheion, gorgieion). The text is structured chronologically by archonship and lists the specific boards of hieropoioi and epistatai responsible for the items during those years.
