AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a financial and administrative record from Delos, likely part of the temple accounts managed by the hieropoioi. It documents the leasing of sacred lands or estates (temple properties) to various individuals. The text details the terms of the lease, the names of the lessees and their guarantors, and provides an inventory of the agricultural assets (vines, fig trees) and structural elements (doors) present on the properties. It also includes accounts related to the sanctuary of Eileithyia, a common feature in Delian administrative epigraphy.
