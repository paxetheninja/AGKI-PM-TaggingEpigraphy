AI Analysis Summary

This inscription from the sanctuary of Apollo at Claros dates to the proconsulship of Juventius Celsus (c. 129/130 AD). It records a religious delegation from Laodicea on the Lycus. The text details a dedication made by Publius Aelius Glycon, a prophet of Pythian Apollo, following an oracular command. It includes a list of civic and religious officials, the names of the boys and girls who performed a hymn to Apollo Clarios, and the signature of the scribe Postuminus.
