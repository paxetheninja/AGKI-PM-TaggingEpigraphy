AI Analysis Summary

This is a significant Athenian honorific decree found on Delos, dating to the mid-2nd century BC (c. 160/159 BC). It documents the diplomatic and financial relationship between Athens and the Kingdom of Pontus, specifically King Pharnaces I and Queen Nysa. The text details the honors granted (crowns and statues), the festivals where these honors are to be proclaimed (Dionysia, Panathenaia, Eleusinia, Ptolemaia), and the administrative steps taken to ensure the decree's execution, including the appointment of a three-man commission and an envoy, Philoxenus of Piraeus.
