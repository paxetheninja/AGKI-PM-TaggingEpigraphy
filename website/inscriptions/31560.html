AI Analysis Summary

This is a significant honorific decree from Gytheion (Laconia) honoring two Roman brothers, Nemerius and Marcus Cloatius. The text provides a detailed account of their benefactions, which include providing loans to the city during financial crises, negotiating with Roman officials and military commanders, and reducing interest on debts. The decree grants them the status of proxenoi and euergetai, along with privileges like proedria (front-row seating) at games. The text is dated by several eponymous magistrates and mentions the involvement of Athenian judges. The inscription was to be set up in the temple of Apollo.
