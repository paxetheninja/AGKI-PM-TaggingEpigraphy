AI Analysis Summary

This inscription from Larisa in Thessaly is a decree of the people (demos) honoring two individuals, Ascalapiades of Pergamum and Philotes of Cyzicus. It dates to approximately 170 BC and mentions significant historical figures including King Eumenes II of Pergamum, his brother Attalus, and a Roman general named Publius. The text begins with a detailed list of local officials in office at the time of the decree and praises the honorees for their virtuous conduct and goodwill toward the citizens.
