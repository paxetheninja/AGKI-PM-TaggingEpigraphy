AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a victor list from an athletic festival in Athens, likely the Theseia or Panathenaia, dating to approximately 130 BC. It records winners in different age categories (first, second, and third age of boys, and men) for various track and combat events. The competitors are primarily Athenian citizens identified by their tribes, though one individual from Smyrna is mentioned, suggesting an open category ('from all').
