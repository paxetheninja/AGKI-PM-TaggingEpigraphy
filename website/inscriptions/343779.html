AI Analysis Summary

This Samian honorary decree dates to the late 4th century BC, following the return of the Samian exiles in 322 BC. It honors Batichos of Cos for his support during the period of exile. The decree grants him proxeny, the title of benefactor, and citizenship, ensuring his integration into the Samian social and political structure (tribes and clans). It also provides him with legal protections and priority access to the council.
