AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a classic Delian temple inventory from the 2nd century BC. It meticulously records the sacred treasures of the sanctuary, including gold and silver vessels and crowns. It is historically significant for mentioning Roman generals like Lucius Cornelius Scipio, reflecting the increasing Roman presence in the Aegean during this period. The text is highly structured, organized by the terms of various archons and treasurers.
