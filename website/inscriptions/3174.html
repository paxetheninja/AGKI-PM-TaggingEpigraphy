AI Analysis Summary

This is an Athenian honorific decree dating to the early 2nd century BC. It honors Pausimachus, son of Philostratus, who had been in the service of King Eumenes. The decree grants him Athenian citizenship (politeia) and an olive crown. It follows the standard administrative procedures of the period, including the involvement of the Council (Boule), the Assembly (Ekklesia), and the scrutiny (dokimasia) by the law-courts (thesmothetai). The mention of the oracle of 'the god' in the reconstruction likely refers to Apollo at Delphi. The publication was funded by the military treasurer and placed on the Acropolis.
