AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a fragment of the 'Attic Stelai' (specifically IG I³ 421), which record the public auction of property confiscated from individuals convicted of the profanation of the Eleusinian Mysteries and the mutilation of the Hermae in 415 BC. The text lists a wide variety of assets, including slaves of various ethnicities, household furniture (like Milesian beds), textiles (from Orchomenus), and agricultural produce (wheat, lentils, oil), along with their sale prices and the 'eponion' (sales tax).
