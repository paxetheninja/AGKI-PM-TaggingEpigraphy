AI Analysis Summary

This inscription records a territorial arbitration (krisis) between the cities of Hermione and Epidaurus, mediated by judges from Kleonai. The text defines a 'common territory' (koine chora) and establishes its physical boundaries using natural landmarks (promontories, water flow) and man-made stone circles (Boleoi). It also regulates agricultural rights such as harvesting (karpeia) and pasturage (epinoma) within this shared land, and mentions a council (synedrion) that holds authority over specific matters like livestock (goats).
