AI Analysis Summary

This inscription from the Roman Imperial period records the regulations for a foundation established by Phaenia Bomation in Gytheion. The endowment was designed to provide a perpetual supply of oil for the gymnasium, benefiting both citizens and resident foreigners. The text is highly technical, outlining the duties of magistrates (strategoi and synedroi), the legal requirements for securing loans from the fund with landed property, and the right of any Greek or Roman citizen to prosecute officials for negligence. It also highlights the social status of the donor's freedmen (apeleutheroi) and foster children (threptoi), ensuring their inclusion in the benefits and protection of the city.
