AI Analysis Summary

The inscription is a decree from the city of Gonnoi in Thessaly, dating to the late 3rd century BC. It concerns the appointment of Nikaios, son of Aristokrates, as a theorodokos (host for sacred ambassadors) for the Athenians. The decree was proposed by Eukles, son of Eukles, of the Attic deme Potamos. It outlines the honors granted to those who host sacred ambassadors for the Eleusinian, Panathenaic, and Mystery festivals, including the status of proxenos. The text also specifies that the names of these hosts should be recorded in the Metroon in Athens and that the decree itself should be inscribed on a stone stele and placed in the temple of Athena in Gonnoi.
