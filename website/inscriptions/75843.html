AI Analysis Summary

This is a formal honorific decree from Thera dating to the mid-2nd century AD (c. 149 AD). It honors Titus Flavius Cleitosthenes Claudianus, a wealthy and distinguished citizen, for his extensive public services and liturgies. The honorand is specifically praised for his voluntary commitment to restore several dilapidated public and religious structures at his own expense, including the Royal Stoa, the Caesareum, and the temples of Eileithyia and Tyche. The decree mentions the involvement of local magistrates and the Roman Proconsul Popillius Priscus, and it mandates the public recording of these honors on a stone stele and the erection of statues.
