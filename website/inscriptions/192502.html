AI Analysis Summary

This inscription from Rhodes, dating after 212 AD, honors Marcus Aurelius Cyrus for his service as a priest of Dionysus Bakcheios. It records his extensive euergetism, including a 20,000 denarii endowment for distributions to the council and citizens on specific birthdays, the provision of oil, and the funding of cultic performances involving a water-organist and hymn-singers. The text also mentions his family members, including his son who was an ephebe.
