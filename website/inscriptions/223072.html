AI Analysis Summary

This document is a decree from the city of Ptolemais Hermiou in Egypt, dated to 104 BC (Year 13 of Cleopatra III and Year 10 of Ptolemy X Alexander I). It addresses the delay in erecting statues for the King and Sarapion, a former gymnasiarch. To fund these honors, the council decides to enroll new citizens into the gymnasium, using their entrance fees (eisodia) for the construction of the bronze statues. It reflects the intersection of civic honors, gymnasium culture, and financial management in late Ptolemaic Egypt.
