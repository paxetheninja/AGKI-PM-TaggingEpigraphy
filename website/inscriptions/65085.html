AI Analysis Summary

This late 2nd-century BC inscription from Delos records the dedication of a theater to Hagne Aphrodite. It was commissioned by Nicostratus, a priest from the Athenian deme of Lamptrai, and a group of cult participants (therapeutai). The dedication is made on behalf of the people of Athens and the people of Rome, highlighting the island's political status. The text is followed by an extensive list of individuals, including cult officials like zakoroi and bastageis, as well as family members of the donors. The latter half of the inscription is heavily damaged with many missing names.
