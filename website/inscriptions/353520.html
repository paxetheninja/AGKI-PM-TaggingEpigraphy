AI Analysis Summary

This mid-4th century BC inscription from Ionia provides a meticulous description of the borders of Thebes at Mykale. It utilizes a sequence of natural features (the Maeander river, various hills), man-made markers (walls, ditches), and religious sites (sanctuaries of Hecate, Apollo, and Athena) to define the territorial limits between the Thebans, Samians, and Prienians.
