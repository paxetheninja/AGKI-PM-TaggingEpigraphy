AI Analysis Summary

This inscription, known as the Erythrae Decree (IG I³ 14), is a foundational document of the First Athenian Confederacy. It records the terms imposed by Athens on Erythrae following a revolt. The decree establishes a democratic council of 120 members, selected by lot, and installs Athenian officials (overseers and a garrison commander) to ensure loyalty. It includes a detailed oath for the new council members, swearing to act in the best interest of the people of Erythrae and the Athenians and their allies, while explicitly forbidding the return of exiles who fled to the Persians (Medes) or the support of tyrants.
