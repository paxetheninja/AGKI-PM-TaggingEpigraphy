AI Analysis Summary

This inscription from the sanctuary of Asclepius at Epidauros is a comprehensive financial account of building activities. It documents payments made to various contractors for architectural features such as doors, windows, altars, and a bathhouse. The text provides valuable data on the costs of materials (iron, bronze, wood, pottery) and labor in the 4th or 3rd century BC, as well as the administrative oversight by the hieromnamones and the council.
