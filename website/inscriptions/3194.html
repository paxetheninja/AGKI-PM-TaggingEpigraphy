AI Analysis Summary

This is an Athenian honorific decree dated to 137/6 BC. It recognizes the religious service of Nicocrates of Phlya, who served as the priest of Asclepius and Hygieia. The text highlights his performance of traditional sacrifices (eisiteteria, bouythysia), his management of festivals like the Asclepieia and Epidauria, and the participation of his children in sacred roles (his daughter as an arrephoros and his son Dion as a key-bearer). The decree was ratified by the council and the people during an assembly held in Piraeus.
