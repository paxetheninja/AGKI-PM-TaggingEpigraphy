AI Analysis Summary

This is a significant Athenian decree from the early 1st century BC (c. 80 BC) honoring the kosmetes Doulos and the ephebes under his charge. It details the religious sacrifices performed on the Acropolis to Athena Polias and other traditional gods, and provides a comprehensive catalogue of the ephebes, including foreign royalty like the Cappadocian kings Ariobarzanes and Ariarathes, who were enrolled as 'Philoromaioi' (Friends of the Romans).
