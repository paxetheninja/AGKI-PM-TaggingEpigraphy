AI Analysis Summary

This inscription, dated to 78 AD, records a formal land survey conducted by Titus Flavius Monimitus, a freedman of the Emperor Vespasian. The document delineates the boundary between Lacedaemon (Sparta) and Messene, listing a series of numbered boundary markers (horoi) and providing precise measurements in feet (podes). Many of these markers were inscribed directly onto natural rock (petrai). The text also references local religious landmarks, including the sanctuaries of Zeus Hypsistos and Artemis Limnatis, and mentions the Eleutherolacones league.
