AI Analysis Summary

This inscription from Delos is a complex administrative document, likely an inventory and account record of the Athenian administration on the island. It details the leasing of sacred lands or properties (indicated by 'misthos' and 'eggye'), lists various ritual and domestic bronze objects (kraters, basins, lamps), and includes an inventory of naval equipment ('skeue tes trierous'). It concludes with a reference to a legal proceeding in the Stoa Poikile involving voting pebbles (psephon).
