AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a Hellenistic honorific decree from Priene. It honors Apellis, son of Nicophon, for his twenty years of service as a secretary, particularly his fourteen years serving the generals without pay and his financial contributions to the city. The decree grants him a golden crown, public announcement at the Dionysia, front-row seating at games, and free meals at the Prytaneion and Panionion. It also provides instructions for the inscription of the decree on a stone stele to be placed in the Sanctuary of Athena Polias, with the costs to be handled by the administrator Pythotimus.
