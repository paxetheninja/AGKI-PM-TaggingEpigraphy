AI Analysis Summary

This inscription consists of multiple Athenian decrees from the archonship of Pythodelos (335/4 BC). It honors Phyleus of Oinoe and his colleagues, who served as hieropoioi (officials in charge of sacred rites), for their conduct and virtue. The text includes standard administrative details such as the prytany, the presiding officers (proedroi), and the secretary, as well as specific instructions for the treasurer to fund a golden crown and a stone stele to be erected on the Acropolis.
