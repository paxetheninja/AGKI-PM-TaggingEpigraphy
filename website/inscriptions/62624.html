AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a comprehensive administrative and financial record from the sanctuary of Apollo on Delos, dated to the archonship of Menekrates (278 BC). It documents the accounts of the hieropoioi, including income from sacred lands (enerosia), rents from sacred houses, interest on loans, and a massive inventory of votive offerings (silver and gold vessels, crowns, and jewelry) stored in various temples. It also lists expenditures for public works, sanctuary personnel, and religious ceremonies, providing a detailed view of the economic life of the sanctuary.
