AI Analysis Summary

This archaic Doric inscription (IvO 22) is a legal document or treaty found at Olympia but concerning the city of Selinous. It regulates the return of exiles, the confiscation of property, and inheritance rights. The presence of an 'aisimnatas' (magistrate/arbitrator) and the mention of Megara (Selinous' mother city) indicate a period of political reconciliation or reorganization. The text is dated by an Olympiad and an eponymous magistrate, Demokrates son of Aristophanes.
