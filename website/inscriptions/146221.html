AI Analysis Summary

This complex inscription from 67 AD contains two distinct parts: the first is the verbatim speech of Emperor Nero at the Isthmian Games in Corinth, where he declared the liberation of Greece from Roman provincial administration and taxation. The second part is a decree from the city of Akraiphia, proposed by the high priest Epameinondas, which grants Nero divine honors, identifying him as Zeus Eleutherios and a 'New Helios'. The stone is a primary witness to the imperial cult and the political history of the 1st century AD, notably showing the erasures of Nero's and Messalina's names following Nero's fall from power.
