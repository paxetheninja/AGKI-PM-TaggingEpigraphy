AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a decree (psephisma) from Lindos on the island of Rhodes, regulating the organization of the Sminthia festival. It outlines the selection process for choragoi (chorus sponsors) for the cult of Dionysus, specifying that both citizens and resident foreigners (including those living or farming in Lindia) are eligible. The decree involves the mastroi (local officials) and the epistatai (overseers), and mentions the religious roles of the priests of Athena and Helios. It also references the Rhodian calendar month of Diosthuos and the submission of official lists of names to the administration.
