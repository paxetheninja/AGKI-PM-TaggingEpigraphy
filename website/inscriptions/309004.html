AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a detailed honorific decree from the deme of Rhamnous, dating to the archonship of Ekphantos (235/234 BC). It honors Dikaiarchos of Thria for his extensive military service as a phrourarchos (garrison commander) across several key Athenian fortresses (Rhamnous, Eleusis, Panakton) and Eretria under the Macedonian kings Antigonus Gonatas and Demetrius II. The text highlights his role in maintaining discipline among soldiers, protecting local populations and livestock during wartime, and his personal financial contributions to religious festivals like the Nemesia when public funds were lacking. It provides significant insight into the military administration of Attica during the Hellenistic period and the relationship between local demes, the city of Athens, and the Macedonian monarchy.
