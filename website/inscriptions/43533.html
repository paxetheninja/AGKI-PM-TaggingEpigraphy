AI Analysis Summary

This inscription from Naupactus contains several records of sacral manumission. Slaves are 'sold' to the god Asclepius at the sanctuary of Krounoi to obtain legal freedom. The records include the names of the sellers, the slaves, the prices paid in silver, guarantors (bebaioteres), and witnesses. Many of these acts include a 'paramone' clause, requiring the freed person to remain in service to a specific individual for a set duration.
