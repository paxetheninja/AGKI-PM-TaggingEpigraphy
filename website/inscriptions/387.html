AI Analysis Summary

This inscription represents the official financial records of the Athenian state for the archonship of Glaukippus (410/409 BCE). It documents the expenditure of sacred funds from the treasury of Athena Polias, which were handed over to the Hellenotamiae to fund various public needs during the Peloponnesian War. These expenses include the maintenance of the cavalry, the diobelia (a state welfare payment), the organization of the Great Panathenaia festival, and military operations involving generals and trierarchs. The text is a primary source for understanding Athenian public finance, administrative structure, and the role of the tribes in the democratic process.
