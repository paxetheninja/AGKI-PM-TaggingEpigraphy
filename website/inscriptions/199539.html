AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a decree from the city of Olous honoring a benefactor, clearly a physician, for his dedicated service during a period of plague and hardship. He is granted citizenship, the title of proxenos, a gold crown, and various privileges such as tax exemption and safe passage. The decree was to be displayed in multiple temples, including those of Zeus Tallaios and Asclepius in Olous, and Apollo Temenitas in Kasos. The text is fragmentary at the beginning where the name of the physician would have been recorded.
