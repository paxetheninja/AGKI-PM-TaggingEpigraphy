AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a classic Attic treasure record from the early 4th century BCE. It documents the annual inventory and transfer of sacred property belonging to Athena Polias and Athena Parthenos. The text is highly formulaic, listing items by material (gold, silver, ivory), weight, and often the name of the original dedicator. It provides significant evidence for the administration of the Athenian Acropolis and the wealth of the city's sanctuaries.
