AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a formal treasury inventory from Athens, dated to 376/5 BC. It documents the transfer of sacred property between outgoing and incoming treasurers (tamiai). The inventory lists precious metal objects dedicated to Demeter and Artemis Brauronia, providing specific weights and descriptions of their condition. The mention of the public seal and the Metroon highlights the official administrative nature of the document.
