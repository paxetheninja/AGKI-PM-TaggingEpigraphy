AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a Hellenistic honorific decree from Kalymna, dating to the late 3rd century BC. It records the military achievements of Lysandros, son of Phoinix, during a conflict with Hierapytna. Lysandros is commended for his leadership of the auxiliary naval forces and his bravery during a naval engagement near Cape Laketer, where he successfully captured enemy combatants. The decree mandates a public crowning and the announcement of his honors during the Great Dionysia festival.
