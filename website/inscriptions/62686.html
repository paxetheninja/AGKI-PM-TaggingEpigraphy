AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a comprehensive administrative and financial record from the island of Delos, dated to the 3rd century BC. It was produced by the hieropoioi, the officials responsible for the administration of the Sanctuary of Apollo. The text includes a detailed account of the sacred treasury's income (from rents of sacred lands and houses, and taxes like the ferry tax to Rheneia) and expenditures (for sacrifices, wood, and temple maintenance). It also features an extensive inventory of dedicated silver vessels (phialai), recording their weight in drachmas and the names of the individuals or groups who dedicated them, often organized by the archonship in which they were received.
