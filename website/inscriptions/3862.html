AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is an Athenian inventory (paradosis) dating to the late 4th century BC (likely 335/4 BC). It records the transfer of various items between outgoing and incoming boards of officials. The list is diverse, encompassing bronze vessels (kraters, chytrai, foot-basins), furniture (tables, couches), and precious metal objects (a gold crown, silver phialai). Notably, it includes naval equipment like trireme rams (emboloi) and items related to sacred festivals, such as prizes (athla) and equipment for sacred sheep. The meticulous recording of weights and the physical condition of the items (e.g., 'broken') reflects the rigorous administrative and financial oversight characteristic of the Athenian state.
