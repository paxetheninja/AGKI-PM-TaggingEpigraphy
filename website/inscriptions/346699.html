AI Analysis Summary

This significant Athenian decree, dating to approximately 120 BC, provides a detailed regulatory framework for the standardization of weights and measures (metrology) in Attica. It establishes official standards to be kept in key locations (the Skias in the Agora, Piraeus, Eleusis, and the Akropolis) and defines the technical specifications for various units like the mina, talent, and choinix. The law distinguishes between commercial and silver weight systems and sets specific measures for various commodities such as nuts, olives, and figs. It also outlines a strict enforcement regime involving various magistrates, the Council of 600, and the Areopagus, with penalties ranging from corporal punishment for slaves to heavy fines for free citizens and officials.
