AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a fragment of a sacred calendar or sacrificial law from Kos. It meticulously details the ritual obligations for several days in the middle of a month, likely Pedageitnyos or Batromios. It specifies the animals (pregnant sheep, heifers, oxen), the deities receiving them, the financial value of the victims, and the specific portions of meat and skins (gerē) that the priests and the Phyleomachidae clan are entitled to. It also mentions ritual prohibitions, such as 'no carrying away' (apophora) of certain remains from the temple.
