AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is an official letter from the Amphictyonic League at Delphi to the Athenian state (Council and People). It confirms the validity of previous decrees and the privileges (asylia, ateleia, and the right to wear gold) granted to the Athenian Guild of Artists (Technitai). The text acknowledges the authority of Roman decrees and the Roman Senate, referring to the Romans as common benefactors. It lists the Athenian ambassadors who facilitated the communication and dates the event to the archonship of Eucleides in Delphi.
