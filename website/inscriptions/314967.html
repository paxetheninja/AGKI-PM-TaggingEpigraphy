AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a military register from Thespiai, dating to the mid-3rd century BC. It records the transition of young men (ephebes) into the ranks of the hoplites and cavalry. The text is dated by the archon of the Boiotian League at Onchestos (Arsiphronidas) and the local archon of Thespiai (Plistos). The list provides a valuable prosopographical record of the citizen body of Thespiai during the Hellenistic period. All names have been transliterated to English/Latinized forms as required.
