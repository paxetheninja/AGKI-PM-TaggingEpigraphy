AI Analysis Summary

This is a formal honorific decree from the city of Chersonesos in the Tauric region, dated to 129/130 AD. It honors a foreigner from Heraclea with citizenship and proxeny. The inscription is highly significant for its detailed list of civic magistrates (archons, nomophylakes, prodikos) and its reference to the goddess Parthenos as the reigning queen of the city, a unique feature of Chersonesan political theology.
