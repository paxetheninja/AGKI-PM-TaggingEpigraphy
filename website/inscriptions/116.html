AI Analysis Summary

This is a fragmentary Athenian decree dating to approximately 410 BC. It honors Evagoras I, the King of Salamis in Cyprus, for his services to Athens. The text mentions the Athenian Council (Boule) and Assembly (Demos), and refers to diplomatic relations involving the Persian King and the island of Chios. The structure is typical of late 5th-century Attic epigraphy, written in the stoichedon style.
