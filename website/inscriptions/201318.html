AI Analysis Summary

This inscription is a formal record of an arbitration process from 118 BC. The city of Knossos acted as a mediator between Lato and Olous to settle a long-standing dispute. The document outlines the magistrates in power at the time, the agreement to submit to Knossian judgment, and the requirement to publish the final decision in major sanctuaries across Crete and on the island of Delos. The text contains several lacunae and reconstructed sections, particularly towards the end.
